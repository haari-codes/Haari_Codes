About Haari_Codes

Learn • Practise • Build

A learning project created to make programming easier to understand, practise and explore, one lesson at a time.

🌱 Our Story

Haari_Codes started with a simple idea: learning programming should feel less confusing and more accessible to beginners.

This project brings lessons, examples and quizzes together in one place so learners can study at their own pace and practise what they learn.

Started: [Add your actual starting date]

Why we started: To make coding concepts easier to learn and help students build confidence through practice.

💻 What You Can Learn

  • C Programming
  • C++ Programming
  • Data Structures
  • SQL
  • HTML and CSS
  • Quizzes and coding practice

Haari_Codes is designed especially for learners who want to strengthen their basics and practise step by step.

I am a student who enjoys learning computer science and explaining concepts in a simple way. I created Haari_Codes to bring learning resources together and help other students practise programming.
