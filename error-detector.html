What Can It Detect?

  • HTML: common missing closing tags, missing attributes and malformed tag patterns.
  • CSS: unbalanced braces, missing declarations and some missing semicolons.
  • JavaScript: common missing brackets, parentheses, unfinished strings and simple syntax patterns.
  • C/C++: common missing semicolons, suspicious missing headers and unmatched brackets.

These are rule-based checks, not a full compiler. Some warnings may be false positives, and some errors may not be detected. Advanced language features, comments, strings and complex syntax can make simple checks imperfect.
